## What is Newsy?

Newsy is a newsletter system heavily inspired by [utteranc.es](https://utteranc.es).
Instead of relying on any external service and maintaining a separate mailing list,
your newsletter lives with your code in your GitHub repository.

## Why GitHub?

Because it's free, and you don't have to bother with email apis, and database security,
handling subscriptions/unsubscriptions, and other newsletter related things to github,
so that you can focus on your content.
